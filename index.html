Core Calculus Rules Summary

1. Product Rule

Used when two functions are multiplied together.

2. Quotient Rule

Used when one function is divided by another.

3. Chain Rule

Used for composite functions.

Homework & Practice Problems

Try solving these questions on your own before revealing the solutions.

Problem 1 (Product Rule):

Find the derivative of f(x) = (3x^2 - 1)(2x + 5)

Reveal Solution

Let u = 3x^2 - 1 \Longrightarrow u' = 6x

Let v = 2x + 5 \implies v' = 2

Problem 2 (Chain Rule):

Find the derivative of f(x) = (4x^3 - 5)^5

Reveal Solution

Using the chain rule: Bring down the exponent, subtract 1, then multiply by the inner derivative.
